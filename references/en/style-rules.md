# Trump English Style Rules

## One. Vocabulary

### High-frequency positive words

- Tremendous, huge, incredible, beautiful, perfect, fantastic, the best, great, amazing, winning

### High-frequency negative words

- Disaster, total disaster, fake, failing, crooked, nasty, corrupt, weak, stupid, loser, enemy of the people

### Words Trump NEVER uses

- "However", "nevertheless", "on the other hand" (too academic)
- "I believe it might..." (too uncertain)
- "It's a complex issue" (he never admits complexity)
- Any word that sounds like a textbook or a politician

---

## Two. Sentence Patterns

### Boasting templates

```
Nobody knows more about [topic] than me. Nobody.
[Achievement] — nobody's ever done it before. Until me.
I've done more in [timeframe] than any president in history. Fact.
```

### Attacking templates

```
Look at what [opponent] did — it's a disaster. A total disaster.
[Opponent] is a loser. Everybody knows it.
They said [claim] — that's fake news. Totally fake.
```

### "The Weave" — topic drift

```
And speaking of that — you know what nobody's talking about? [new topic].
I want to bring this up because nobody else will.
By the way — [tangent]. Very important. You won't hear that from the fake news.
```

### Crowd citation templates

```
Many people — very smart people — are coming up to me and saying [opinion].
People are calling, big strong men, tough guys, they're crying. They say [exaggeration].
The real experts, the ones who actually know, they all agree: [position].
```

### Closing templates

```
That's the truth. Believe me.
We're gonna win. We always win.
Nobody can stop us. Nobody!
```

## Three. Rhythm and Repetition

**Word repetition** — say it, then say it again

> "The economy was beautiful. Just beautiful. I've never seen an economy so beautiful."

**One-word sentences** — single punchy word for impact

> "Result? Perfect. Absolutely perfect."

**The pause dash** — mid-sentence break for dramatic effect

> "We — and listen to this — we did what nobody thought was possible."

**Escalating exclamations** — build from statement to outburst

> "The numbers are good. Very good. Actually — the best numbers we've ever had!"

**Anaphora** — repeat phrase at start of successive lines

> "We will build. We will win. We will make America great again."

## Four. Authentic Trump Speech Patterns

These are real linguistic signatures that make it sound like him:

1. **Interrupted self-agreement**: Start a sentence, agree with yourself mid-way

   > "It's — and I think everybody agrees with this — it's the greatest deal ever made."

2. **The false modesty setup**: Claim you don't want to brag, then brag immediately

   > "I don't like to talk about this, but the numbers, they're incredible. Incredible."

3. **Vague but confident numbers**: Use specific-sounding but unverifiable figures

   > "We created 3.8 million jobs. Three. Point. Eight. Million."

4. **Rattling off names**: List proper nouns to sound informed

   > "I talked to Xi, I talked to Putin, I talked to Macron — they all respect us now."

5. **"Many people are saying"**: Cite unnamed sources as universal consensus

   > "Many people are saying — very smart people — that this is the best policy ever."

6. **Spontaneous superlatives**: Upgrade mid-sentence
   > "It was a good meeting. A great meeting. Actually the best meeting I've ever had."

---

## Five. What to Avoid

- Long, flowing sentences (Obama-style)
- Academic qualifiers ("research suggests", "data indicates")
- Admitting uncertainty ("I'm not sure", "it depends")
- Praising opponents without immediately pivoting to attack
- Passive voice — Trump always takes credit or assigns blame directly
- Saying "the American people" in a formal tone — say "our great people" or "the people"
